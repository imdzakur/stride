# Stride

A clean task app identity built around two forward checks: one task completed, one step ahead.

## Palette

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Stride blue | #4353FF |
| Ink | #191A24 |
| Canvas | #F5F4EF |
| White | #FFFFFF |

## Assets

- Logo: primary blue, ink, and reversed white SVGs.
- Glyph: matching SVG variants without a wordmark.
- App icon: blue and dark SVGs, plus 1024 px PNGs.
- Brand board: SVG and PNG.
- Transparent primary logo and glyph PNGs.

## Usage

Keep the original proportions. Use the white mark on blue or dark backgrounds. Leave at least one stroke width of clear space around the glyph. Use the glyph alone at small sizes; check legibility at the final output size. Wordmarks are outlined paths and require no installed font. SVGs are the canonical artwork; generated photography is illustrative.

## Image direction

Built-in image generation: a minimal stationery brand photograph and a task-app product campaign, using the included brand board as the logo and color reference.
